# How To Use
Often times when you try to upload a asset (sticker or emoji), it will get blocked due to being over Discord's size limit. This tool allows you to simply upload an asset than change the size of it! Keep in mind that the quality of the asset will often change the more you compress it.

To upload the asset, you can either click or drag the file you want to upload into the box and the tool will automatically set up the editor.
