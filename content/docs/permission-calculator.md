# How To Use
This tool allows you to calculate the permission integer for your applications! To begin, simply use a preset or search and find all the permissions your app needs to have! We recommend only selecting ones your app actually uses as you can always update it later! Once you have done that, paste your bot's id into the "Client ID" field and copy the OAuth2 Invite Link button! If you just want the integer, you can just click the "copy" button!
