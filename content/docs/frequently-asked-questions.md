# Frequently Asked Questions
A list of questions that get commonly asked! If your question is not here, feel free to check out our other guides!

**Q: Is this project open source?**
- A: Yes! Both the main site and docs are open source.

**Q: Is anything paywalled?**
- A: Nope! Everything is fully free and can be used by anyone.

**Q: I have a problem, what do I do?**
- A: If you join our Discord server, we have a channel dedicated to answering any questions you may have.

**Q: If I want to help, where do I go?**
- A: You can help in a variety of ways! You can help by sharing ideas and suggestions, donating to the project, or contributing to our repos.

**Q: What's the catch?**
- A: There are no catches! The whole point of the project is to provide every feature rich tool for free and everything we do is aimed at making that goal closer and closer.

**Q: Is there an API?**
- A: There is no API at this time. We do plan on adding publicly useable APIs in the future to give applications more options however that is still a work in progress and no ETA is available.
